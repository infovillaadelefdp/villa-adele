Data Deletion Request

Villa Adele · User Data Removal

If you have interacted with Villa Adele through Facebook or Instagram and would like to request the deletion of any personal data we may hold, please follow the steps below.

How to request data deletion:

Send an email to info.villa.adele.fdp@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and include your name and the Facebook or Instagram account associated with your request.

We will process your request within 30 days and confirm deletion by reply email.

Please note that Villa Adele uses the Meta API solely to publish content to our own Facebook Page and Instagram account. We do not collect, store, or process personal data from third-party Facebook or Instagram users beyond standard page interaction metrics provided by Meta.

For further information, see our Privacy Policy.
